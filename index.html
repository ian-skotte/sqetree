Special Qualifying Event Decision Tree

Use this guide to help determine whether a request may qualify as a special qualifying event due to loss of eligibility for other coverage or acquisition of a new dependent.

Start Here

Is the request due to one of these events?
↓

Loss of eligibility for other coverage

Use this path when an employee or dependent lost eligibility for other group insurance coverage, TennCare or CHIP.

Acquisition of a new dependent

Use this path for marriage, birth, adoption, placement for adoption or court-ordered legal guardianship.

If neither event applies, the request may not qualify as an SQE. Review whether another qualifying event applies or whether the change must wait until Annual Enrollment.

Was the loss voluntary?
Yes

This is not an SQE for loss of eligibility.

  • Voluntary cancellation does not qualify.
  • Coverage canceled for nonpayment of premiums does not qualify.
  • Choosing to waive, decline or cancel coverage during another plan’s enrollment period does not qualify.
No

Continue to determine who lost eligibility.

↓
Who lost eligibility?

Employee lost eligibility

If the employee meets the loss-of-eligibility requirements, the employee may enroll. Eligible dependents may also be added at that time.

Dependent lost eligibility

If only a dependent lost eligibility, only that dependent may enroll. Other dependents may enroll only if they also lost eligibility at the same time.

The enrollment opportunity follows the person who experienced the qualifying loss.
Important: If the employee did not lose eligibility, the employee does not automatically gain enrollment rights just because a dependent lost coverage.
Who lost eligibility? Who may enroll? Reminder
Employee Employee and eligible dependents The qualifying event is tied to the employee.
One dependent Only that dependent The employee does not gain enrollment rights from the dependent’s loss.
Multiple dependents Only dependents who lost coverage at the same time Other dependents may not be added unless they also lost eligibility.
Coverage type reminder: The person may only enroll in the type of coverage lost. For example, loss of medical coverage allows medical enrollment; loss of dental coverage allows dental enrollment; loss of vision coverage allows vision enrollment.
Was the completed application and required documentation received within 60 days of the loss?
Yes

Coverage begins the first day of the month after the completed application and required documentation are received.

No

The request should be denied as untimely.

Path 2: Acquisition of a New Dependent

Did the employee acquire a new dependent?
No

This path does not apply. Review whether another qualifying event applies.

Yes

Continue based on how the dependent was acquired.

Event Deadline Effective date guidance Additional reminder
Marriage Within 60 days First day of the month after the completed application and documentation are received. Employee may enroll in employee-only or family coverage and may add the new dependent and other eligible dependents.
Legal guardianship Within 60 days First day of the month after the completed application and documentation are received. The change must correspond with the guardianship order. Employee-only coverage is not permitted.
Birth Preferably within 30 days; no later than 60 days If received within 30 days, health coverage may be retroactive to the date of birth. If received 31-60 days after the event, coverage begins the first day of the following month. Dental and vision coverage begin the first day of the month after the request is received.
Adoption or placement for adoption Preferably within 30 days; no later than 60 days If received within 30 days, health coverage may be retroactive to the date of adoption or placement. If received 31-60 days after the event, coverage begins the first day of the following month. Dental and vision coverage begin the first day of the month after the request is received.
Timing reminder: Requests submitted after the allowed enrollment period should be denied as untimely.

Quick ABC Checklist

Question Why it matters
What event caused the request? Determines whether this is a loss-of-eligibility event or new-dependent event.
Who experienced the event? Determines who may enroll.
Was the loss voluntary? Voluntary loss of coverage does not qualify as an SQE.
Was the request submitted on time? Most deadlines are 60 days; birth, adoption and placement have special 30-day retroactive rules.
Was required documentation submitted? The application is not complete until required documentation is received.
What type of coverage was lost? The person may only enroll in the type of coverage lost.
Premium reminder: Premiums are not prorated. If coverage is approved, the member must pay the premium for the full month in which coverage begins.
